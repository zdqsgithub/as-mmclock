# v13 Mouse Methylation Clock Model Card

Date: 2026-05-19

## Model Purpose

This project predicts chronological age from mouse methylation region features.
The current v13 benchmark is valid only inside the support-covered regime
defined below.

## Accepted Use

Use the current model benchmark for support-covered chronological-age
prediction.

A held-out prediction is support-covered when:

- train data contains at least `10` same-tissue samples;
- held-out age is no more than `8` weeks above the train same-tissue maximum
  age;
- dataset, tissue, age, and intervention metadata are available and traceable.

## Current Metrics

- Support-covered headline MAE: `20.286` weeks over `15573` rows.
- Unsupported stress-test MAE: `36.659` weeks over `7620` rows.
- `GSE121141 old104+ brain_cortex/heart/lung` stress MAE: `88.565` weeks over
  `420` rows.
- Best observed CR AUC: `0.8744`, research-level held-out validation only.

## Required Stress-Test Reporting

`GSE121141 old104+ brain_cortex/heart/lung` remains unresolved. It must be
reported as a stress-test/blocker metric, not as the current headline pass/fail
benchmark.

Unsupported predictions may be used for diagnosis, but they must not be used to
justify autoresearch or broad model claims.

## Biological-Age / CR Scope

CR metrics are research-level validation only. They must come from real
held-out predictions and must be reported with shuffled-intervention sanity
when available.

Do not infer biological age from metadata-only labels or placeholder metrics.

## Out of Scope

- Full-lifespan old brain_cortex/heart/lung generalization.
- Clinical or translational claims.
- Claims that GSE121141 old104+ failure is solved.
- Human clock CpG mapping.
- Dummy CR/rapamycin AUC.
- Autoresearch on unsupported old104+ metrics.
- Raw FASTQ/Bismark work without a separate minimal ETL approval.
- Treating v21 raw pilot or AutoDL package scaffolding as a production model
  update before raw matrix gates, sanity checks, and interpretation reports pass.

## Use Checklist

Before using the model result as a headline metric:

- Confirm the sample is support-covered by same tissue and age range.
- Confirm the input matrix comes from a validated methylation matrix pipeline.
- Confirm `sample_id`, `dataset_batch`, `tissue`, and age metadata are present.
- Report unsupported predictions separately as stress-test or diagnostic rows.
- Keep CR/biological-age statements limited to research-level held-out
  validation.
- For v21 exploratory reports, distinguish age-associated methylation signal
  from aging mechanism and label dataset, tissue, and coverage confounding.
